StreamClip AI – Terms of Service

Effective date: 21 November 2026

What StreamClip AI is

StreamClip AI is a personal, non-commercial tool. One person, the operator, runs it on their own computer. It makes short clips from the operator's own stream recordings and publishes them to social media accounts the operator owns and has connected. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up.

Acceptable use

  • It is used only with content the operator owns or has the rights to publish.
  • It posts only to the operator's own accounts, through each platform's official API. It never uses scraping or browser automation.
  • All use follows the terms and community guidelines of TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, including their content-sharing and branded-content rules.
  • Publishing is off by default. Posts go out only after the operator switches publishing on and approves them.

Connected accounts

Accounts are connected through the platform's own sign-in (OAuth). StreamClip AI only keeps the access the platform grants, and the operator can disconnect at any time in the app or in the platform's settings.
