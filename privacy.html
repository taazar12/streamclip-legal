StreamClip AI – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

StreamClip AI is a personal tool run by one person, the operator, on their own computer. It turns the operator's own stream recordings into short clips and posts them only to social media accounts the operator owns and has connected. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign up for it.

What data it handles

  • The operator's own videos and clips. These are stored on the operator's computer.
  • Connected account data. When the operator connects TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, the platform sends back an access token and basic profile details (account ID and display name). On TikTok, it also returns the posting options the account allows. If view counts are collected, the app also reads the views and likes of its own posts.
  • Clip text. This includes spoken words transcribed from a clip, titles, captions and hashtags.

StreamClip AI does not collect data about viewers or other users, does not use cookies or tracking, and runs no ads.

How it is used

The data is used only to make clips and to publish them, with their captions, to the operator's own connected accounts through each platform's official API. Nothing is posted until the operator turns publishing on.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored locally on the operator's computer. Access tokens are encrypted at rest. They are never shown in the app's interface and never written to logs.

Sharing

Data is never sold, rented or shared for advertising. It goes to only two kinds of outside service:

  • The platforms the operator posts to (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram). They receive the video and its caption when a clip is published.
  • Google Gemini. It receives clip transcripts and a few still frames, which are used to suggest titles, captions and which moments to clip. Google handles that data under its own terms.

Disconnecting and deleting data

  • To disconnect an account, use Settings → Publishing accounts → Disconnect in the app. This deletes the stored token. Access can also be removed in the platform's own settings, for example under TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Deleting clips or the app's data folder permanently removes the videos and their text from the operator's computer.
  • Posts already published stay on the platform until they are deleted there.

Children

StreamClip AI is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.
