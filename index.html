Your best stream moments, clipped and posted.

StreamClip AI finds the highlights in your own stream recordings, cuts them into vertical clips with a facecam split, captions and a headline, and posts them to your own TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels accounts.

Finds the moments

It listens and watches for big reactions, laughs and plays, then trims each one into a clean short.

Ready-to-post format

9:16 layout with facecam on top, gameplay below, word-by-word captions and a headline.

You stay in control

Nothing is posted until you approve it. Accounts are connected through each platform's official sign-in and can be disconnected at any time.

How it works

  1. Upload a recording of your own stream.
  2. Review the clips, captions and hashtags StreamClip AI suggests.
  3. Connect your accounts and publish the clips you approve.
